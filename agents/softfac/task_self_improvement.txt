Continue the SoftFac v2 implementation from the current migration checkpoint.

Authoritative execution contract:

1. Astra creates one immutable whole-project master plan. Hermes selects READY
   tasks by stable plan order and dependency state; ordinary commits, repairs,
   and resume do not call Astra again.
2. AGY edits only a disposable non-Git mirror. The boundary validates the
   complete delta and atomically syncs allowed changes to the host-owned
   candidate worktree. Source, Git metadata, and trusted state stay protected.
3. Runner owns deterministic verification, scope/integrity gates, staging, and
   `git write-tree`. Sol independently reviews the exact
   `{base_commit,candidate_id}` candidate. Hermes alone may commit.
4. Runner FAIL and Sol REJECT route to AGY repair. Repeated stagnant or
   exhausted repair attempts become BLOCKED with complete evidence; Luna is
   outside the v1 production path.
5. Resume preserves completed commits, candidate identity, evidence, and
   attempt budgets. If a pending commit cannot be reconciled exactly, fail
   closed rather than replaying or guessing.
6. Keep tests deterministic and separate offline fake-provider proof from live
   authenticated runtime proof. Do not introduce a scheduler, registry,
   duplicate state database, or unrelated provider abstraction.
