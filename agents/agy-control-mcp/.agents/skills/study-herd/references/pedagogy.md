# Pedagogy contract

Teach a topic in this order:

1. State the slide line or claim.
2. Restate it in clear Vietnamese.
3. Build the prerequisite anatomy, physiology, pathology, or pharmacology.
4. Explain cause -> mechanism -> consequence.
5. Connect it to the clinical and exam consequence.
6. Mark common traps and distinctions from current practice when relevant.
7. Ask one novel application question with decisive data.

Use difficulty levels: recall, mechanism, comparison, clinical one-best-answer, and multi-step/ambiguous case. Move forward after demonstrated transfer across different contexts. Do not confuse one remembered fact with mastery.

Do not create Anki or flashcards until the learner says `học xong` or `kết thúc`.
